## Changes to A that must be preserved

- Rich supervision may include binary, type, region/mask, attribute judgment, attribute-region binding and explicit context/relations. Each field needs authority, reveal time and cost; normal images do not imply every attribute is absent.
- Multiple anomalies can coexist, including in one region. A correction to abrasion cannot erase contaminant evidence. A combined anomaly mask does not identify which region belongs to which attribute.
- A1 applicability is regional, attribute-specific and contextual; A2 may update a lightweight shared head; A3 audits shared/local responsibility. Independent state writes do not automatically guarantee independent behavior, especially with shared normalization, routing or eviction.
- Context can be read broadly while writes remain evidence-specific. Product ID must not become a hidden answer. Visually distant means different appearance, not an unobservable blurred image.
- Fewer questions is assessed at comparable verified reliability and cost. Distinguish rate targets from budget ceilings, budget exhaustion from learning, and diagnostics from actual closed-loop savings. Do not silently restore forced warm-up queries.
- Small stable benefits, error-propagation control or human-cost savings can be meaningful. Freeze the main contribution/metric path before examining candidate outcomes; never switch endpoints to rescue an unsuccessful registered claim.
